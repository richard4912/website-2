No request received. Proceeding anyway.
OUTCOME
Held. Indefinitely.

The window faces south. This was established by lying in it for four hours and noting that the situation improved.

Occupation began at 0900 and has not lapsed since, except for meals, and once for a noise downstairs that turned out to be nothing.

A rival surface was evaluated on the second day: the radiator shelf. Warmer, but narrow, and it does not have the view. The file remains open.

No permission was sought. There is nobody to seek it from, which the bureau has decided to treat as approval.

This is the first issue. There was no first issue before it, which is the only claim in this publication that can be checked.
