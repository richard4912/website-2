OUTCOME
Morale increased beyond measurable limits.

Agent 002 weighs eight grams and arrived without paperwork, an interview, or any indication of having been asked.

Attempts to establish who hired her have been unproductive. The roster was amended to include her on the grounds that she was already on it.

Her specialisms are weather, branches, and the merits of sitting still. Agent 001 covers rooms, furniture and food. The overlap is zero, which nobody planned.

A working arrangement has formed. It was never drafted and has no end date, because nobody proposed one and nobody asked.

The masthead now claims two agents on duty. This issue exists largely to make that claim true in print.
